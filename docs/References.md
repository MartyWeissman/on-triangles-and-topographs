# References and relation to prior work

Kisao Takeuchi, *Arithmetic triangle groups*, **J. Math. Soc. Japan** 29
(1977), no. 1, 91–106. [DOI: 10.2969/jmsj/02910091](https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/02910091).

Theorem 3 (§5, pp. 105–106) classifies arithmetic triangle groups. All 25 triples
in this project occur among its compact types. The present classification is
therefore a subset of Takeuchi’s list, not a new classification of all arithmetic
triangle groups. This comparison records the relationship to the literature;
Takeuchi’s theorem is not a premise of the Lean proof or itself formalized here.
